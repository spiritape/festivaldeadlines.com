Privacy

Effective 12 October 2026

Festival Deadlines collects no data

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. We don't know who you are, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your starred festivals, your submission ticks and your filters are stored on your device and never leave it.

What the app downloads

The app downloads its festival calendar from our server, and fetches each festival's logo from that festival's own website. Like any web request, those sites can see your device's IP address. That is the app's only network activity.
